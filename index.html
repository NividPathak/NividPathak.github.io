01 · Multi-agent AI · May 2026 – Present

JARVIS

A personal AI assistant where a router sends each voice or text request to the right specialist agent, running on local LLMs.

Approach

Specialist agents for calendar, weather, news and sports run in parallel on local LLMs through Ollama, with a swappable Anthropic API backend and an on-device voice pipeline (wake word + Whisper).

Fine-tuned a 4-bit Llama 3.2 3B with LoRA on MLX as the router, replacing a Llama 3.1 8B baseline.

Result

The smaller fine-tuned router was faster and scored higher on held-out requests.

0.885 → 0.947router macro-F1
2.3x fasterrouting latency, 857 → 372 ms
What held up

A McNemar's exact test on 94 test examples confirmed the speed gain, but the accuracy gain is not significant yet (p = 0.302), so I report it that way.

02 · Causal inference · Jan 2026 – Apr 2026

Minimum Wage Causal Study

Do large state minimum wage increases raise low-end wages, and do they cost food-service jobs?

Approach

Started as a four-person course project on 739K BLS wage records: an ETL pipeline, wage prediction models (R² = 0.97) and a Streamlit dashboard.

After the course I extended it on my own into a causal study of state minimum wage increases from 2005 to 2022, using Callaway-Sant'Anna difference-in-differences, a synthetic control of Arizona's 2017 increase, pre-trend and placebo tests, and a simulation power analysis.

Result

Large increases raised 10th-percentile wages by about 9.4% (ATT 0.090, 95% CI 0.068 to 0.113).

+9.4%10th-percentile wages
p = 0.048Arizona permutation test, rank 1 of 21
What didn't hold

The food-service job-loss estimate didn't hold up once the pandemic years were excluded (-0.034 shrank to -0.005, p = 0.62), so I report it as not robust.

03 · Data engineering + ML · Oct 2026 – Present In progress

RTD Delay Radar

A streaming lakehouse on Databricks that predicts how late Denver RTD buses and trains will be at their upcoming stops.

Approach

A Python collector polls RTD's live GTFS-Realtime feeds every 60 seconds. Spark Structured Streaming and Auto Loader turn the raw data into bronze, silver and gold Delta tables, with data quality checks in Lakeflow pipelines.

Experiments and models are tracked in MLflow, deployment runs through Databricks Asset Bundles and GitHub Actions, and an AI/BI dashboard shows live delays alongside model accuracy.

How it's judged

The model predicts stop-level arrival delays and is benchmarked against RTD's own predictions and a persistence baseline that assumes current delays carry forward.

Status

The collector has been running since October 2026. Results will be posted once enough history builds up.
